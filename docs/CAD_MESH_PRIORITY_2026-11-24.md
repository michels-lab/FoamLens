### Scientific workflow extensions after CAD/mesh
- Solidification: threshold-defined liquid/mushy/solid volumes, front surfaces and arrival times where temporal coverage supports it; do not label a threshold as a physically verified front. Distinguish native alphaL from 1-alphaL and from VOF phase fractions.
- Flow: reuse existing glyph/vector/streamline tools; later time-dependent particle paths with measured time history and explicit termination outside domain. Instantaneous streamlines are not transient particle trajectories.
- Interface heat transfer: compare local q distributions and integrated power, both signs and area-weighted reciprocity; cumulative energy from measured sample times, report initial interval uncertainty and distinguish sensible/latent inventories. No double counting energyFlux and its components.
- Contraction/free surface: conditional on imported actual phase fraction, mesh motion/displacement, density or surface data. Monitor volume, interface position and topology over time; identify unresolved gaps and do not infer shrinkage from solidification fraction alone.
- Thermocouple validation and thesis exports: selected physical XYZ probes, experimental/simulation overlays, coverage/units, RMSE and provenance. Reuse existing mapping and reproducible export.
- UX: context-specific CAD/mesh inspector (source tree, central view, collapsible properties/selection) with linked chart/table on demand; no permanently duplicated global toolbar, no clipped velocity or source selectors, and no forced horizontal scrolling.
